How it works

Three steps from curiosity to a staffed research desk.

1

Name a beat 🎯

A geography + a sector. Ask for a "general researcher" and the Master politely refuses — a junior that covers everything knows nothing. Geography-only gets a generalist, with a clear no-sector-depth warning.

2

Sharp intake 🧵

Three quick asks — your focus themes or tickers, your time horizon, your reporting cadence — so the junior comes out crisp about exactly what you asked for. Watch it hatch, absorb its knowledge, and fly into your nav.

3

Deep dives on demand 📊

Chat with each junior directly: live market reads on verified tickers, a research agenda built from your brief, and news / filings / macro feeds as the platform lights them up. Every source's wiring is stated honestly.
